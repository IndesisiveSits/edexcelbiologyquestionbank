How it works
Choose Combined question paper + mark scheme when both are in one PDF. SNAB Vault detects mark-scheme pages from their headings, then separates numbered answer sections. PDF pages are rendered as images so diagrams, tables and original formatting are retained. Automatic detection groups subparts into whole numbered questions, including questions that continue across pages. Scanned/image-only PDFs may need OCR and can fall back to full-page crops. Source PDFs and question images are stored in your private Supabase cloud bucket. Specification points use Pearson Edexcel Biology A (SNAB, 9BN0) Topic 1–8 numbering. Auto-matches are suggestions; check them before exporting. Open Pearson specification.
